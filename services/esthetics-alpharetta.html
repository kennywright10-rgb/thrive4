Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between medical esthetics and a regular facial?

Medical esthetics uses physician-supervised protocols, clinical-grade devices, and professional-strength products. The results are more significant and more consistent than what's available at a standard spa.

Which treatment is right for me?

HydraFacial is ideal for hydration, extractions, and instant glow with zero downtime. Microneedling is best for collagen, scars, and anti-aging. We'll recommend the best option during your free skin consultation.

Can I combine these with other THRIVE 4 services?

Absolutely. Many clients pair esthetics with IV therapy for skin nutrition, red light therapy for collagen support, or hyperbaric oxygen to accelerate post-treatment healing.

Do I need a consultation first?

We always do a brief skin assessment before your first visit. It takes just a few minutes and ensures your treatment is customized to your skin type and goals.

Are these treatments safe for all skin tones?

Yes. HydraFacial and our core esthetics treatments are safe across all Fitzpatrick skin types. Microneedling is also safe for all tones when performed by trained professionals.
