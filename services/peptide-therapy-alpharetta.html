What Is Peptide Modality?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids (2–50 amino acids) that function as signaling molecules throughout your body. Unlike medications that block or inhibit, peptides tell your cells to do more of what they should naturally be doing — produce growth hormone, heal tissue, reduce inflammation, or improve neurological function. Each peptide is highly specific, targeting particular receptors and triggering precise cellular responses. You get targeted benefits without the systemic side effects of broader pharmaceutical interventions.

How are peptides administered?
