What to Expect

1

Numb & Prep

Topical numbing cream is applied 30–45 minutes before your session so you're completely comfortable throughout.

2

Treatment (30–45 min)

Your provider uses a medical-grade device across your skin. Needle depth is customized per zone. A nourishing serum is applied simultaneously.

3

Heal & Glow

Mild redness for 24–48 hours, then your skin heals and produces new collagen. Full results from each session appear at 4–6 weeks.

Who Should Consider Microneedling?

Ideal Candidates

  • Acne scarring — One of the most effective non-surgical options available
  • Fine lines & skin laxity — Restore firmness without fillers or toxins
  • Hyperpigmentation — Sun damage, dark spots, and melasma
  • Enlarged pores — Visible pore size and rough texture
  • All skin tones — Safe for Fitzpatrick skin types I–VI

Cautions & Contraindications

  • Active acne breakouts — Defer until skin is clear
  • Blood-thinning medications — Discuss with our your doctor first
  • Keloid scarring history — Requires medical evaluation
  • Pregnancy — Not recommended
  • Recent Accutane use — Wait 6 months after stopping

Our medical team reviews your health history before every session to ensure safety and optimal outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions do I need?

Most clients see great results after 3–6 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. For acne scars, 6+ sessions may be needed. Maintenance every 3–6 months keeps results looking fresh.

Is it painful?

Numbing cream is applied before treatment, making it very comfortable for most clients. Most rate discomfort at 1–3 out of 10.

What's the downtime?

Redness for 24–48 hours, similar to a mild sunburn. Most clients return to normal activities the next day. Avoid sun and apply SPF for the first week.

What skin concerns can microneedling treat?

Fine lines, acne scars, hyperpigmentation, enlarged pores, uneven texture, and dullness. It works on all skin tones — one of the most versatile treatments available.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. Microneedling pairs beautifully with hyaluronic acid and growth factor serums applied through the channels. Our team will recommend the right combination for your goals.
