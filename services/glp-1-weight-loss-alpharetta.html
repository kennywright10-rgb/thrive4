What Is GLP-1 Therapy?

GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) is a naturally occurring hormone your gut releases after eating. It signals your brain that you're full, slows digestion, regulates blood sugar, and reduces appetite. GLP-1 receptor agonists — medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide — mimic and amplify this signal, making it dramatically easier to eat less, feel satisfied longer, and lose weight consistently.

These are the same active compounds behind widely discussed medications like Ozempic and Wegovy, administered here under physician supervision as part of a personalized, medically managed program — not a telehealth checkbox.
