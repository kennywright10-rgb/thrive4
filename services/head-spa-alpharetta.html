What Happens During a Head Spa

1

Scalp Analysis

Your specialist examines scalp condition — dryness, oiliness, sensitivity, and buildup — to customize your treatment and select the right serums.

2

Cleanse & Exfoliate

A professional-grade shampoo removes product buildup, excess sebum, and environmental residue. Gentle exfoliation clears dead skin cells from hair follicles.

3

Steam & Serum Infusion

Warm steam opens the scalp's pores while a targeted serum — hydration, growth, or repair-focused — is massaged in for maximum absorption.

4

Therapeutic Massage

The heart of the experience. A rhythmic, tension-releasing scalp massage that also covers the neck, shoulders, and pressure points for total relaxation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a head spa take?

Sessions run 60–90 minutes. You'll get scalp analysis, cleansing, exfoliation, steam treatment, serum, and a full massage — it goes by fast.

Can a head spa help with hair loss?

Yes. Scalp massage increases blood circulation to hair follicles, and targeted serums can reduce inflammation — two key factors in thinning. Regular sessions support a healthier scalp environment for growth.

How often should I come in?

Monthly for scalp health and hair goals. Every 2–4 weeks for ongoing stress management. There's really no limit — it's completely safe to enjoy regularly.

Do I need to prepare anything beforehand?

No prep needed. Come with dry or freshly washed hair — either is fine. We'll handle the rest, and you'll leave with clean, nourished hair.

Is this just a fancy shampoo?

Not at all. A head spa is a full clinical treatment — professional scalp analysis, therapeutic serums, steam therapy, and skilled massage techniques you won't find at a regular salon.
