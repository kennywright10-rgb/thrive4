What Is HOCATT Therapy?

HOCATT stands for Hyperthermic Ozone and Carbonic Acid Transdermal Therapy. At its core, it's a specialized full-body chamber — similar in appearance to an egg-shaped sauna capsule — that combines up to ten distinct therapeutic modalities delivered together in a carefully sequenced 30-minute session.

You sit comfortably inside the chamber with your body enclosed and your head outside, breathing clean room air throughout. This means you receive the full benefit of the internal therapies while your head and lungs remain completely clear of ozone exposure. A session at THRIVE begins with a brief clinical intake to tailor your settings, and a trained member of our team monitors you throughout.

The combination of therapies creates synergistic effects that are greater than any single modality alone — which is why patients and physicians describe HOCATT as one of the most efficient wellness investments available.

The 10 Therapies Inside One HOCATT Session

1. Transdermal Ozone Therapy

Ozone (O₃) is delivered through the skin, reaching the bloodstream and tissues without passing through the lungs. Ozone is a powerful oxidant that neutralizes pathogens (bacteria, viruses, fungi), reduces inflammation, stimulates the immune system, and triggers antioxidant defenses at the cellular level. Transdermal delivery is safe and effective — and at THRIVE, it's physician-calibrated to your tolerance and health goals.

2. Carbonic Acid Therapy

The session begins with a phase of carbonic acid infusion. Carbonic acid causes blood vessels to dilate and pores to open, dramatically increasing oxygen delivery to peripheral tissues through a mechanism known as the Bohr Effect. This sets the stage for everything that follows — priming the body to absorb the ozone and infrared therapies more effectively.

3. Far Infrared Sauna

Deep-penetrating infrared heat warms tissue from the inside out — reaching muscles, joints, and organs at a cellular level that surface heat cannot achieve. Far infrared promotes detoxification through sweat, increases circulation, reduces muscle soreness and joint stiffness, and elevates core temperature to support the immune-activating effects of hyperthermia.

4. Steam Hyperthermia

Elevating core temperature in a controlled way mimics the body's fever response — activating white blood cell production, killing heat-sensitive pathogens, and stimulating systemic immune activation. This is one of the oldest evidence-supported wellness modalities, now delivered with precision.

5. PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field Therapy)

PEMF delivers pulsed electromagnetic energy to cells, restoring their natural electrical charge and stimulating cellular repair. Research supports PEMF for reducing inflammation, supporting bone health, accelerating tissue recovery, and improving cellular energy production at the mitochondrial level.

6. Photon Light / Chromotherapy

Specific wavelengths of light delivered within the chamber support skin rejuvenation, mood regulation, and cellular repair. Photobiomodulation (red and near-infrared light) is one of the fastest-growing areas of wellness and clinical research, with well-documented effects on collagen synthesis, inflammation, and mitochondrial function.

7. Electrotherapy / Microcurrents

Low-level electrical stimulation targets neuromuscular function, improves local circulation, and reduces pain signals. Microcurrent therapy is used clinically for pain management, wound healing, and facial rejuvenation.

8. Aromatherapy

Essential oils are delivered through the steam environment, providing both aromatic benefits (relaxation, mental clarity, respiratory support) and potential transdermal absorption of therapeutic plant compounds.

9. High-Intensity Pulsed Electromagnetic Fields

A higher-intensity variant of PEMF targeting deeper tissue layers and systemic cellular stimulation — particularly relevant for patients focused on recovery, anti-aging, and immune optimization.

10. Oxygen Breathing (Optional)

Patients may breathe supplemental oxygen through a nasal cannula during the session. This increases arterial oxygen saturation and, combined with the carbonic acid vasodilation phase, significantly amplifies oxygen delivery to every tissue in the body.

What to Expect at Your HOCATT Session at THRIVE

Before your session:

You'll complete a brief intake form and consult with our clinical team about your health history and goals. Settings are calibrated to your profile before every session.

During your session:

You'll change into a bathing suit or be provided a towel, and step into the HOCATT chamber. Your head remains outside, breathing normal room air. The session begins with the carbonic acid phase (vasodilation and pore opening), transitions into the primary ozone and infrared phase, and ends with a gradual wind-down. Most patients describe the experience as warm, relaxing, and deeply comfortable. The session runs approximately 30 minutes.

After your session:

Most patients feel clear, calm, and energized. Some patients — particularly on their first session or if beginning a detox protocol — experience mild fatigue as the body processes released toxins. Hydration is important before and after each session. We recommend drinking water and resting for 15–20 minutes afterward.

Session frequency:

Most patients begin with a series of 3–5 sessions to establish therapeutic baseline. Maintenance frequency varies from weekly to monthly depending on goals and protocol. Your THRIVE physician will guide your recommended cadence.
