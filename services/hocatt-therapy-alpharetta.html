What Is HOCATT Therapy?

HOCATT stands for Hyperthermic Ozone and Carbonic Acid Transdermal Therapy. It is a specialized, full-body chamber system that combines up to ten distinct therapeutic modalities delivered simultaneously in a single 30-minute session. Unlike most wellness therapies that target one mechanism, HOCATT creates synergistic effects by layering ozone, carbonic acid, far infrared heat, PEMF, photon light, electrotherapy, hyperthermia, and optional oxygen breathing into a precisely sequenced protocol.

You sit comfortably inside the chamber with your body fully enclosed and your head outside, breathing clean room air throughout. This design means you receive the complete therapeutic benefit of the internal modalities while your lungs and head remain completely clear of ozone exposure. At THRIVE 4 Peak Performance, every session begins with a clinical intake and is monitored throughout by our trained team.

The combination of therapies working together creates effects greater than any single modality alone — which is why patients and experts describe HOCATT as one of the most efficient wellness investments available. What Happens During a HOCATT Session? The 10 Modalities Explained

1. Transdermal Ozone Therapy

Ozone (O₃) is delivered through the skin in a controlled manner, reaching the bloodstream and tissues without passing through the lungs. Ozone is a potent oxidative molecule that neutralizes pathogens (bacteria, viruses, fungi), reduces chronic inflammation, stimulates the immune system, and triggers antioxidant defenses at the cellular level. At THRIVE, ozone concentration is calibrated precisely to your tolerance and health goals, making transdermal delivery both safe and highly effective.

2. Carbonic Acid Therapy

The HOCATT session begins with carbonic acid (CO₂) infusion. Carbonic acid causes blood vessels to dilate and pores to open significantly, dramatically increasing oxygen delivery to peripheral tissues through a well-documented mechanism known as the Bohr Effect. This phase primes the body, creating optimal conditions for absorption of the ozone, infrared heat, and all subsequent therapies that follow.

3. Far Infrared Sauna

Deep-penetrating infrared rays warm tissue from the inside out, reaching muscles, joints, and organs at a cellular level that surface heat cannot achieve. Far infrared sauna promotes detoxification through enhanced sweat production, increases circulation, reduces muscle soreness and joint stiffness, and elevates core temperature to amplify the immune-activating effects of hyperthermia throughout the session.

4. Steam Hyperthermia

Elevating core temperature in a controlled, therapeutic manner mimics the body's natural fever response — activating white blood cell production, eliminating heat-sensitive pathogens, and triggering systemic immune activation. Hyperthermia is one of the oldest evidence-supported wellness modalities, now delivered with precision timing and safety monitoring through the HOCATT chamber.

5. PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field Therapy)

Pulsed electromagnetic energy is delivered to cells, restoring their natural electrical charge and stimulating cellular repair mechanisms. Research supports PEMF for reducing inflammation, supporting bone density, accelerating tissue recovery, and improving cellular ATP production at the mitochondrial level — the energy powerhouse of every cell.

6. Photon Light / Chromotherapy

Specific wavelengths of light delivered within the chamber support skin rejuvenation, mood regulation, and cellular repair. Photobiomodulation (red and near-infrared light therapy) is one of the fastest-growing areas of clinical and longevity research, with well-documented effects on collagen synthesis, inflammation reduction, and mitochondrial function.

7. Frequency-Specific Electrotherapy / Microcurrents

Low-level electrical stimulation targets neuromuscular function, improves local circulation, and modulates pain signals. Microcurrent therapy is used clinically for pain management, wound healing, and facial rejuvenation — making it a valuable addition to the HOCATT protocol for patients managing chronic discomfort.

8. Aromatherapy with Essential Oils

Therapeutic essential oils are delivered through the steam environment, providing both aromatic benefits (relaxation, mental clarity, respiratory support) and potential transdermal absorption of bioactive plant compounds. Aromatherapy enhances the relaxation response and complements the detoxification pathways activated by the other modalities.

9. High-Intensity Pulsed Electromagnetic Fields

A higher-intensity variant of PEMF therapy targets deeper tissue layers and delivers systemic cellular stimulation. This modality is particularly valuable for patients focused on recovery acceleration, anti-aging, and comprehensive immune optimization.

10. Oxygen Breathing (Optional)

Patients may breathe supplemental oxygen through a nasal cannula during the session. This increases arterial oxygen saturation and, when combined with the carbonic acid vasodilation phase, significantly amplifies oxygen delivery to every tissue in the body. Optional oxygen breathing is tailored to individual health goals and tolerance.
