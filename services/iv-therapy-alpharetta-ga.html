Who Should Try IV Therapy?

Ideal Candidates

  • Athletes and active individuals — Accelerate recovery, replenish electrolytes, and sustain peak output
  • Chronic fatigue or low energy — Direct nutrient delivery bypasses digestive absorption issues
  • Frequent travelers — Rehydrate and rebuild immunity after flights and time zone shifts
  • Weakened immune system — High-dose vitamin C and zinc protocols support immune defense
  • Chronic stress or burnout — Replenish what stress depletes — B vitamins, magnesium, and antioxidants
  • Migraine or headache sufferers — Magnesium IV therapy has strong clinical evidence for migraine relief

Contraindications & Cautions

  • Kidney disease — Fluid and mineral load may be contraindicated without physician clearance
  • Heart failure or fluid overload conditions — IV fluids can worsen fluid retention; requires medical evaluation
  • Pregnancy — Most formulas require physician evaluation before use
  • Allergy to IV components — Ingredient review required; rare but possible reactions to vitamins or additives

Medical screening is required before your first session — we'll confirm eligibility at your consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an IV therapy session take?

Most IV infusions take 30-45 minutes. NAD+ therapy may take 2-4 hours depending on dosage.

Does IV therapy hurt?

You'll feel a small pinch when the IV is inserted. Most patients find it no more uncomfortable than a blood draw. Once the IV is in, you can relax, work on your laptop, or scroll your phone.

How often should I get IV therapy?

Frequency depends on your goals. Many patients start with weekly sessions, then move to bi-weekly or monthly maintenance. Our physicians provide personalized recommendations.

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, when administered by medical professionals. We use pharmaceutical-grade ingredients, sterile equipment, and screen all patients for contraindications before treatment.

Does insurance cover IV therapy?

Most IV therapy is considered elective wellness and not covered by insurance. We provide itemized receipts for HSA/FSA reimbursement and can assist with insurance submission.
