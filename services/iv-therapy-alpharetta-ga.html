Who Should Try IV "Drip"?

Ideal Candidates

  • Athletes and active individuals — Accelerate recovery, replenish electrolytes, and sustain peak output
  • Chronic fatigue or low energy — Direct nutrient delivery bypasses digestive absorption issues
  • Frequent travelers — Rehydrate and rebuild immunity after flights and time zone shifts
  • Weakened immune system — High-dose vitamin C and zinc protocols support immune defense
  • Chronic stress or burnout — Replenish what stress depletes — B vitamins, magnesium, and antioxidants
  • Migraine or headache sufferers — Magnesium IV "Drip" has strong clinical evidence for migraine relief

Contraindications & Cautions

  • Kidney disease — Fluid and mineral load may be contraindicated without medical clearance
  • Heart failure or fluid overload conditions — IV fluids can worsen fluid retention; requires medical evaluation
  • Pregnancy — Most formulas require medical evaluation before use
  • Allergy to IV components — Ingredient review required; rare but possible reactions to vitamins or additives

Medical screening is required before your first session — we'll confirm eligibility at your consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an IV "Drip" session take?

Most IV infusions take 30-45 minutes. NAD+ modality may take 2-4 hours depending on dosage.

Does IV "Drip" hurt?

You'll feel a small pinch when the IV is inserted. Most clients find it no more uncomfortable than a blood draw. Once the IV is in, you can relax, work on your laptop, or scroll your phone.

How often should I get IV "Drip"?

Frequency depends on your goals. Many clients start with weekly sessions, then move to bi-weekly or monthly maintenance. our team provides personalized recommendations.

Is IV "Drip" safe?

Yes, when administered by medical professionals. We use pharmaceutical-grade ingredients, sterile equipment, and screen all clients for contraindications before service.

Does insurance cover IV "Drip"?

Most IV "Drip" is considered elective wellness and not covered by insurance. We provide itemized receipts for HSA/FSA reimbursement and can assist with insurance submission.
