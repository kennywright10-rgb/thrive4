What Is Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Technology (MHBOT)?

Mild Hyperbaric oxygen modality (MHBOT) works on a simple but profound principle: pressure forces oxygen into your bloodstream far beyond what normal breathing achieves. Inside our pressurized chamber, oxygen concentration in your plasma increases up to 1,000% — reaching tissues that normally receive inadequate oxygen due to poor circulation, injury, or inflammation.

That oxygen surge activates healing pathways throughout the body: new blood vessels form (angiogenesis), stem cells mobilize, and anti-inflammatory proteins increase. The results range from dramatically faster recovery from injury and surgery to measurable improvements in brain function, energy levels, and immune resilience.

Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Modality — FAQ

What is Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Technology?

MHBOT involves breathing approximately 90–93% oxygen in a pressurized chamber at 1.3 ATA. The oxygen in MHBOT is not medical-grade (100% pure oxygen) as it is not a medical modality — it is a wellness technology designed to support performance, recovery, and general wellbeing.

How long is each session?

Typically 60–90 minutes, during which you breathe oxygen comfortably in the chamber. You can read, listen to music, or rest.
