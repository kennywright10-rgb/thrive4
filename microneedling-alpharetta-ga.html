The Microneedling Process at THRIVE Alpharetta

1

Cleanse & Numb

We cleanse your skin and apply a medical-grade topical numbing cream. We wait 20–30 minutes to ensure you're comfortable before starting.

2

Precision Microneedling

A sterile microneedling pen creates thousands of controlled micro-channels in the skin, triggering your body's natural collagen-building response.

3

Serum Infusion

With channels open, growth factors, hyaluronic acid, or peptide serums are applied — absorbing 300x deeper than topical application alone.

4

Calm & Protect

We finish with a calming mask and SPF. You'll be pink for 24–48 hours, then see progressive improvement over 4–8 weeks as collagen builds.

Microneedling Alpharetta — Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions do I need?

Most clients see meaningful results in 3–6 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. We'll recommend a protocol based on your specific concerns at your first visit.

What's the downtime like?

Expect 24–48 hours of redness and mild sensitivity — like a light sunburn. Most clients return to normal activity the next day. Full results continue building for 4–8 weeks as collagen remodels.

Is microneedling safe for darker skin tones?

Yes — unlike laser resurfacing, microneedling is safe across all Fitzpatrick skin types with minimal risk of hyperpigmentation. It's one of the best resurfacing options for Alpharetta's diverse population.

Does it hurt?

We apply medical-grade numbing cream before every session. Most clients describe a mild sensation — very tolerable. We adjust needle depth based on your comfort level.
