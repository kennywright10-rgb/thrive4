We use your information to:

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

We do not share your phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Types of Messages: You may receive text messages from THRIVE 4 Peak Performance including appointment reminders, booking confirmations, promotional offers, wellness updates, and general information about our services.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your appointment schedule and preferences. You may receive up to 4–8 messages per month.

Message and Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier plan. THRIVE 4 Peak Performance is not responsible for any charges imposed by your carrier.

How to Opt In: You may opt in to receive SMS messages by submitting your phone number via our website contact or booking form, by texting a keyword to our designated number, or by providing verbal or written consent when scheduling an appointment in person or by phone.

How to Opt Out: You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. You may re-subscribe at any time by replying START.
