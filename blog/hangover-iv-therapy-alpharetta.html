Here's a physician's breakdown of what's actually happening in your body after a night of drinking, what's in a proper hangover IV drip, and realistically how fast you can expect to feel better.

Why a Hangover Hits So Hard

Alcohol is a diuretic — it blocks the release of antidiuretic hormone (ADH), which causes your kidneys to pull more water out of your bloodstream than usual. For every alcoholic drink, you can lose meaningfully more fluid than you took in, which is why you wake up dehydrated even if you drank plenty of water the night before.

At the same time, alcohol metabolism burns through your body's stores of B vitamins and magnesium, disrupts blood sugar regulation, and generates acetaldehyde — a toxic byproduct that's largely responsible for the headache, nausea, and brain fog of a classic hangover. Add in a night of poor sleep quality (alcohol disrupts REM sleep even when it helps you fall asleep faster), and you have the full hangover picture: dehydration, electrolyte loss, inflammation, low blood sugar, and sleep debt, all at once.

What's In a Hangover IV Drip

IV

Isotonic Fluids

A liter or more of balanced IV fluids rehydrates you far faster and more completely than drinking water, which has to be absorbed through the gut — often a slow process when you're nauseated. IV fluids go straight into the bloodstream, restoring blood volume and easing the pounding headache that comes with dehydration almost immediately.

Mg

Magnesium

Magnesium is depleted by alcohol and plays a direct role in relaxing blood vessels and calming the nervous system — both of which help take the edge off a hangover headache and the jittery, wrung-out feeling that follows a big night.

B

B-Complex + B12

B vitamins are heavily used up during alcohol metabolism. Replenishing them supports the liver's detoxification pathways and helps restore the energy and mental clarity that a hangover wipes out.

C

Vitamin C & Antioxidants

High-dose Vitamin C helps counter the oxidative stress created by alcohol byproducts and supports immune function — useful if a late night is compounded by travel, poor sleep, or a busy week ahead.

Rx

Anti-Nausea & Anti-Inflammatory Medication (Optional)

For patients with significant nausea or headache, our physicians can add anti-nausea medication or an anti-inflammatory to the protocol. This is determined during your pre-treatment assessment based on your symptoms and health history.

How Fast Does It Work?

Most patients start feeling noticeably better within 30 to 45 minutes — often before the infusion is even finished. Because IV fluids and nutrients bypass digestion entirely, rehydration and electrolyte correction happen far faster than anything you could achieve orally, especially if nausea is making it hard to keep food or water down.

A full hangover IV session typically takes 45 to 60 minutes, including a brief physician assessment beforehand. Most patients leave with significantly reduced headache, less nausea, and a real boost in energy — enough to salvage the rest of the day.

One advantage of getting IV therapy at a physician-led clinic rather than a pop-up drip bar: every session starts with a real health assessment. Severe dehydration, alcohol poisoning, and other conditions can sometimes mimic a "bad hangover," and it's important to rule those out before treatment.
