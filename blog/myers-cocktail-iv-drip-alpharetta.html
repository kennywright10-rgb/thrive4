What Is the Myers' Cocktail?

The Myers' Cocktail is an intravenous nutrient infusion combining magnesium, calcium, B vitamins (including B12 and B complex), and Vitamin C delivered directly into the bloodstream via slow IV push or drip. The formulation bypasses the gut entirely — which is why it achieves tissue concentrations that oral supplementation simply cannot match.

When you take oral vitamins, absorption is limited by GI transport proteins, first-pass liver metabolism, and individual variations in digestive health. IV delivery sidesteps all of that. A standard Myers' Cocktail can raise plasma Vitamin C concentrations to levels 50–100× higher than oral supplementation is capable of achieving — and delivers magnesium into cells in a form that's immediately bioavailable.

The Ingredients and What Each Does

Mg

Magnesium Chloride or Sulfate

Magnesium participates in over 300 enzymatic reactions in the body, including ATP (energy) production, muscle relaxation, nerve conduction, and cardiovascular regulation. Studies estimate that more than 50% of adults in the U.S. are deficient. IV magnesium produces rapid smooth muscle relaxation — making it particularly effective for acute migraine, muscle cramping, bronchospasm, and anxiety. It's the workhorse of the Myers' Cocktail formula.

C

High-Dose Vitamin C

At therapeutic IV doses, Vitamin C acts as a potent antioxidant, immune activator, and collagen synthesis cofactor. It's been studied extensively for reducing the duration of upper respiratory infections, supporting recovery from physical stress, and reducing oxidative damage. At the concentrations achievable via IV, it also has documented effects on immune cell activity that oral doses cannot replicate.

B

B-Complex + B12

The B vitamins are the master regulators of cellular energy metabolism. B12 (methylcobalamin or hydroxocobalamin) is critical for neurological function, red blood cell production, and methylation pathways. B-complex (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6) supports mitochondrial energy production and neurotransmitter synthesis. B12 deficiency is common — especially in vegetarians, people on metformin, and adults over 50 — and its effects (fatigue, brain fog, mood changes) are often mistaken for other conditions.

Ca

Calcium Gluconate

Calcium plays a key role in muscle contraction, nerve signaling, and cardiovascular function. In the Myers' formula, it acts synergistically with magnesium — the two minerals work in tandem to regulate muscle tone, nerve excitability, and cardiac rhythm. Together they create the smooth, relaxing effect that many patients notice during and after the infusion.

Who Benefits Most From a Myers' Cocktail?

The Myers' Cocktail has a broad therapeutic window — it's both a powerful treatment for specific conditions and an effective wellness tool for healthy high-performers. The following patient profiles see the strongest results:

Chronic Fatigue and Low Energy

Persistent fatigue is one of the most common reasons patients seek out IV therapy. Magnesium and B12 deficiencies are frequent contributors to fatigue that don't resolve with rest, and both respond rapidly to IV delivery. Many patients report a meaningful energy shift within 24 hours of their first infusion — with compounding benefit over a series of treatments.

Migraine and Tension Headache

IV magnesium has one of the strongest evidence bases in the Myers' Cocktail literature for acute migraine relief. A number of randomized trials have shown IV magnesium to be effective for aborting acute migraine and reducing headache frequency with regular administration. For patients who experience frequent migraines, a regular Myers' protocol can significantly reduce episode frequency.

Athletic Recovery and Performance

High-intensity training depletes magnesium and B vitamins faster than most athletes can replace through diet. Heavy sweating alone can deplete magnesium at a rate that outpaces dietary intake. IV replenishment allows for rapid normalization that supports faster muscle recovery, reduced soreness, and better sleep quality. THRIVE pairs Myers' infusions with our cryotherapy and other recovery services for a comprehensive athlete recovery protocol.

Immune Support and Illness Recovery

High-dose Vitamin C and the B vitamins play direct roles in immune cell production and function. Whether you're in the early stages of a cold, recovering from illness, or preparing for a period of high stress (travel, deadlines, seasonal change), a Myers' infusion provides an immune system top-up that oral supplements cannot match in speed or bioavailability.

Stress, Anxiety, and Burnout

Chronic psychological stress depletes magnesium and B vitamins at an accelerated rate. The magnesium in a Myers' infusion has direct anxiolytic effects through its action on NMDA receptors and the HPA (stress) axis. Patients managing high workloads, chronic stress, or burnout often describe a feeling of physical calm and mental clarity following treatment — effects that are consistent with the pharmacology of IV magnesium.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Myers' Cocktail different from other IV drips?

The Myers' Cocktail is the original physician-developed IV nutrient formula — a specific combination of magnesium, calcium, B vitamins, and Vitamin C. Other IV drips (NAD+, glutathione pushes, high-dose Vitamin C, hydration bags) have different compositions and indications. Our physicians can help you determine which formulation matches your specific goals and health history.

How often should I get a Myers' Cocktail?

Frequency depends on your goals. For acute conditions (migraine, illness, recovery from intense training), a single infusion often produces meaningful results. For ongoing wellness, energy, and immune support, monthly infusions tend to produce compounding benefits over time. Your physician at THRIVE will recommend an appropriate cadence based on your specific situation.

Is the Myers' Cocktail safe?

The Myers' Cocktail has a well-established safety profile and has been administered to millions of patients over several decades. At THRIVE, all IV therapy is physician-supervised and preceded by a health assessment. Contraindications are rare but include certain kidney conditions and specific medication interactions — your physician will screen for these prior to treatment.
