When to Choose Hyperbaric Oxygen Modality

HBOT is the stronger choice when:

  • You're recovering from a significant injury, surgery, or tissue damage
  • You have a condition with poor local circulation — HBOT reaches tissues that topical or localized services cannot
  • You're dealing with a brain health concern (post-concussion, cognitive decline, neurological fatigue including post-COVID symptoms)
  • You want systemic anti-inflammatory effects throughout the entire body
  • You're in an intensive recovery protocol and have the time for longer sessions

Common HBOT Use Cases at THRIVE

Athletes recovering from bone stress injuries, soft tissue damage, or surgery. Clients managing persistent inflammation from autoimmune conditions. Clients seeking neuroprotective support. Longevity-focused individuals using HBOT as part of a cellular aging protocol.

When to Choose Red Light Modality

Red light modality is the better fit when:

  • You want to target specific areas — a joint, a muscle group, the face and skin
  • Your primary goals are skin health, collagen production, or anti-aging
  • You're looking for daily or high-frequency maintenance modality (short sessions, easy to schedule)
  • You want to reduce localized joint pain or muscle soreness
  • You're supplementing another primary recovery modality

Common RLT Use Cases at THRIVE

Clients managing chronic joint pain. Anyone wanting consistent skin improvement (tone, texture, reduced fine lines). Athletes using RLT as a daily recovery supplement alongside cryotherapy or compression. Clients using RLT to support mitochondrial health as part of a broader longevity stack.

Can You Do Both?

Yes — and many of our clients do. HBOT and RLT work through entirely different mechanisms, so there's no redundancy. They complement each other well, particularly for clients managing complex inflammatory conditions, athletes during heavy training cycles, or longevity-focused clients building a comprehensive protocol.

A typical combined protocol might look like: 60-minute HBOT session 2–3 times per week, with a 15-minute RLT session on non-HBOT days. Our team can design a specific schedule based on your goals.

The Honest Answer

If you have the time and the goal is systemic recovery, injury healing, or neurological support — start with HBOT. If you want a fast, targeted, daily-use tool for cellular energy, skin, and localized inflammation — red light modality delivers excellent results with minimal time investment.
