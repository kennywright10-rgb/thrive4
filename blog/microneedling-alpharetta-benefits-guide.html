Microneedling is one of the most researched, results-driven treatments in medical esthetics — and one of the most misunderstood. Patients often picture something painful or dramatic, when in reality it is a well-tolerated, THRIVE Tech supervised treatment with real, measurable effects on skin texture, tone, and firmness.

Here is a breakdown of how microneedling works, what results actually look like, and what to expect before, during, and after a session at THRIVE.

What Is Microneedling?

Microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy, uses a device with fine, sterile needles to create thousands of controlled micro-injuries in the top layers of skin. Those micro-injuries trigger your body's natural wound-healing cascade — prompting fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin, the structural proteins responsible for firm, smooth, youthful-looking skin.

Unlike lasers or chemical peels, microneedling does not remove or burn skin tissue. It works with your body's own repair process, which makes it appropriate for most skin tones and types with minimal risk of pigment changes.

Why Microneedling Works

The benefits come from your body's own collagen remodeling response, which continues working for weeks after each session.

New Collagen and Elastin Production

Controlled micro-injury stimulates fibroblasts to lay down fresh collagen over the following weeks, gradually improving firmness and elasticity.

Improved Texture and Pore Appearance

As new collagen fills in, rough texture, enlarged pores, and fine lines soften noticeably over a series of sessions.

Reduced Appearance of Scars

Microneedling is one of the best-studied treatments for acne scarring and other textural scars, helping to break up scar tissue bands and rebuild healthier collagen underneath.

More Even Skin Tone

By supporting healthier skin turnover, microneedling can help fade mild sun damage and post-inflammatory discoloration over time.

Enhanced Product Absorption

The micro-channels created during treatment temporarily improve absorption of targeted serums applied immediately afterward, amplifying results.

What to Expect During Your Session

  • Consultation and skin assessment: Our team reviews your skin goals, history, and any contraindications before treatment.
  • Cleansing and numbing: Skin is thoroughly cleansed, and a topical numbing cream is applied for comfort.
  • Microneedling pass: The device is moved systematically across the treatment area, adjusting depth based on the area and your skin's needs.
  • Targeted serum application: A soothing, skin-supportive serum is applied to the freshly treated skin to calm and support healing.
  • Aftercare guidance: You leave with specific instructions on sun protection and gentle skincare for the days that follow.

Most sessions take 30 to 45 minutes from start to finish, and discomfort is generally mild thanks to the numbing step.

Downtime and Results Timeline

Timeframe What to Expect
0–48 hours Redness similar to a mild sunburn, mild sensitivity or tightness
2–5 days Redness fades, light flaking or dryness possible
1–2 weeks Skin appears brighter, texture starts to feel smoother
4–6 weeks Visible improvement in firmness and texture as new collagen builds
3–6 sessions Cumulative results in tone, texture, and scar appearance

Because collagen remodeling is gradual, most patients complete a series of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, followed by periodic maintenance sessions to sustain results.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Microneedling?

Microneedling is well suited for patients dealing with fine lines, uneven texture, enlarged pores, mild acne scarring, or early signs of sun damage. Because it does not rely on heat or light-based energy, it is generally appropriate across a wide range of skin tones under proper medical supervision.

Patients with active acne breakouts, certain skin infections, or specific medical conditions should discuss timing with our clinical team before scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is microneedling and how does it work?

It uses fine, sterile needles to create controlled micro-injuries that trigger your body's natural collagen and elastin production for firmer, smoother, more even-toned skin.

How much downtime does it require?

Most patients see redness similar to a mild sunburn for 24 to 48 hours, with light flaking possible for a few days. Most return to normal activities the next day.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients see meaningful results from a series of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, with maintenance sessions afterward.

Can it be combined with other THRIVE treatments?

Yes. It pairs well with HydraFacial for combined exfoliation and collagen stimulation, and many patients support results with IV therapy or NAD+.
