You can spend $200 a month on the highest-quality oral supplements and still be running a significant deficit. The reason isn't the quality of the product — it's the delivery method. Your digestive system, designed to extract nutrients from whole foods over hours of digestion, is simply not optimized for the concentrated doses found in supplements. IV therapy changes this equation entirely. By delivering vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and hydration directly into your bloodstream, IV infusions achieve near-100% bioavailability. What takes your gut hours — with significant losses — happens in 30 to 60 minutes with full absorption guaranteed.

The Bioavailability Problem With Oral Supplements

Bioavailability is the percentage of a nutrient that actually enters your bloodstream and becomes available for your cells to use. For most oral vitamins and minerals, this number is disappointing:

  • Oral Vitamin C tops out at roughly 70–90% absorption at low doses, dropping to under 50% at higher therapeutic doses due to intestinal saturation
  • Oral magnesium supplements achieve 30–40% absorption depending on form, with excess causing digestive distress
  • B12 oral absorption drops below 2% in people with low intrinsic factor — a common issue in older adults and anyone with GI inflammation
  • Glutathione (the body's master antioxidant) is almost entirely broken down by stomach acid before it reaches the bloodstream

IV therapy bypasses all of this. Nutrients go directly from the IV bag into your vein and circulate to every cell in your body within minutes. There is no first-pass metabolism, no intestinal transport limitation, no stomach acid to contend with.
